Crinkle Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Crinkle is developed by Jia Shen. Crinkle works without signing in or creating an account. Your app information stays on your device, and we do not operate servers that receive your Screen Time data or app history.

Information stored on your device

Crinkle stores information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Your selected apps and activity preferences.
  • Screen Time activity totals used to calculate daily results.
  • Your history, achievements, and streaks.
  • Your settings and customizations, such as your cat’s name.

This information is processed locally and shared between Crinkle and its related app extensions on your device so that monitoring, reports, and other features can work.

Screen Time access

With your permission, Crinkle uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks, including Family Controls and Device Activity, to monitor activity for the apps you select and display activity reports.

Crinkle uses this information only to provide its Screen Time features. We do not receive your Screen Time data, use it for advertising, or sell or share it with data brokers.

You can revoke Crinkle’s Screen Time access in your device’s Settings. Features that depend on this permission will stop working when access is revoked.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Crinkle may schedule local reminders and activity notifications on your device. You can turn these off at any time in Settings.

Advertising and analytics

Crinkle does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Storage, backups, and deletion

Crinkle retains its locally stored information while the app is installed, unless that information is reset or removed.

To remove Crinkle’s local app data, delete the app from your device rather than offloading it. Crinkle does not delete the Screen Time records maintained separately by iOS.

Depending on your device settings and Apple’s backup features, Crinkle’s locally stored data may be included in device backups. We do not provide a backup or recovery service and cannot guarantee that your data can be restored. We cannot access or delete your device backups.

Purchases

If you purchase Crinkle or an optional paid feature through the App Store, Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Contacting support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request and resolve support issues, and retain it only as needed for those purposes or to meet legal obligations.

Please avoid sending sensitive information or Screen Time screenshots unless they are needed to explain your issue. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Crinkle’s features or privacy practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
